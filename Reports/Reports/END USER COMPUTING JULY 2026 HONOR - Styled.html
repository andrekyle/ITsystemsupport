Conclusion

Overall, the End User Computing programme made satisfactory progress during July 2026. Four scheduled sessions gave the 16 learners practical exposure to Microsoft Office Excel and helped strengthen the spreadsheet skills required for Module 7. Training was delivered face-to-face, with interactive activities and paired work.

Attendance and participation

Most learners attended three of the four sessions and showed steady participation throughout the month. The overall attendance rate still requires continued monitoring, together with punctuality and timely communication when learners are unable to attend.

Assessment and follow-up

Learners submitted work and are currently moving through the assessment process. Assessment outcomes, outstanding requirements, and individual learner progress should continue to be followed up consistently.

Ongoing facilitator support, together with improved attendance and communication, will help learners address any remaining gaps and continue progressing towards successful achievement of the programme outcomes.
