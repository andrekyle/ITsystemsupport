TIPS FOR GENERATING
- Generate at 16:9 / widest available size; the lesson figures are landscape.
- AI generators cannot spell reliably. If a prompt asks for labels and the text
  comes out garbled, regenerate with 'no text, no labels' and treat the image as
  illustration only (the caption under the placeholder already explains it).
- For anything marked SEARCH, the real photograph will always look better and is
  historically accurate - Wikimedia Commons images of NASA, US Army and pre-1930
  works are public domain.
- Downloads upload straight into the lesson placeholders; images are resized
  automatically and shared to all learner accounts.
