Summary

The August 2026 reporting period reflects encouraging progress by the EUC learners as they move closer to completing their qualification. Learners continued to strengthen their technical knowledge and practical skills in areas such as server administration, peripherals, and Database Access. The cohort showed commitment to learning, with portfolios and assessment submissions continuing through the required processes.

Participation and development

Many learners participated well in class discussions, practical activities, and group exercises, which contributed positively to the learning environment. Teamwork, peer support, and respectful engagement remained strong throughout the month. Several learners also maintained good attendance and showed consistent dedication to their studies and personal development. Overall, the group has shown growth in confidence, technical ability, and workplace readiness since the start of the programme.

Focus through completion

Attendance and punctuality, however, still require attention for some learners. In certain cases, missed sessions and limited communication affected learning continuity and participation. Despite these challenges, learners have remained committed to completing the programme.

As the programme moves into its final stages, attention will remain on completing outstanding activities, logbooks, and assessment requirements. Continued facilitator follow-up and learner support will be important to keep learners engaged and guide them successfully through to completion.

Conclusion

The August training period showed steady progress across the End User Computing programme. Learners continued with Module 7, with particular focus on Microsoft Office Excel. Learners submitted work that is currently moving through the assessment process. Participation in class was generally positive among learners who attended regularly, with several showing good commitment through full attendance and active involvement.

Continued attention

Attendance across the group, however, remained uneven and will require continued attention, together with facilitator intervention where necessary. Communication around absences was inconsistent in some cases. Regular follow-up will therefore remain important to improve attendance, communication, and learner responsibility.

Path to completion

Outstanding assessment and programme requirements will continue to be monitored closely. Learners who remain active should receive the necessary support to complete their outstanding work successfully. Overall, the programme remains on track, with continued monitoring and follow-up required as learners move closer to completion.

Programme status: On track, with continued learner support, monitoring and follow-up required.
