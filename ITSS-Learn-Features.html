ITSS Learn

Platform capabilities overview

What the platform is capable of: a single web app that can teach, assess, mark, track, message, report and audit — automatically, on any device, with bank-grade data privacy.

Teaching & delivery capabilities

  1. Interactive lesson engine — slide-based lessons with images, pop-up glossary definitions, worked examples and collapsible sections
  2. Classroom presenter mode — full-screen slides with speaker notes for teaching the same material live
  3. Knowledge gating — can lock the next slide until the learner answers checkpoint questions correctly
  4. Content management — staff can upload slide decks, images and lesson figures that sync to every device

Assessment & AI-marking capabilities

  1. Auto-marking of 4 question types — multiple choice, select-all, drag-to-order and matching, with instant scoring, attempt history and best-score tracking
  2. AI marking of written answers — a two-stage marker: key-idea detection places a tick on the exact sentence that earned each mark, then a large language model reviews rejected answers for meaning, so learners who answer in their own words still get credit
  3. Explanatory feedback — can tell a learner which idea is missing and quote the exact lesson line that teaches it
  4. Attempt limiting & locking — marked attempts are capped; self-assessments lock after saving with a staff-only unlock
  5. Answer transparency — staff can open any learner and see every option they picked and every sentence they typed

Progress-tracking capabilities

  1. Automatic credit of completed work — taking a quiz, passing an exercise or uploading evidence updates completion instantly; no manual capture anywhere
  2. Dual accounting — a lenient progress view for coaching and a strict, auditable view for awarding credits
  3. Gamification engine — XP, 8 levels and badges computed purely from real recorded work; a live class leaderboard that cannot be inflated

Evidence & document capabilities

  1. Cloud file storage — learners upload evidence (photos, PDFs, documents) from any device; files live in private cloud storage
  2. One-click ZIP export of a learner’s entire portfolio for external moderation
  3. Paper-form digitisation — pixel-accurate replicas of official paper forms (registration form, attendance register) that auto-fill from stored data and print exactly like the originals
  4. Bulk PDF output — print any register, form or report to PDF straight from the browser, including every register at once

Communication capabilities

  1. Private 1-to-1 messaging with online presence, read receipts, message editing and per-sender notifications
  2. One-to-many broadcast — a single message privately delivered to every learner
  3. Q&A and announcements — class-wide questions with threaded replies
  4. Conversation moderation — authorised staff can open any conversation read-only; enforced by the database, not the app

Analytics & reporting capabilities

  1. Live cohort dashboard — completion, quiz & exercise averages, attendance rate, last-seen and XP for every learner in one sortable table
  2. Early-warning engine — automatically flags at-risk learners and states the reasons (low completion, poor attendance, inactivity, low scores)
  3. CSV export of the full analytics table for external reporting
  4. Embedded management reports — formal report documents servable inside the app, restricted to the Super User

Security, privacy & audit capabilities

  1. Row-level security — privacy enforced in the database itself: a learner physically cannot query another learner’s messages, files or records, even outside the app
  2. 5-tier role system — Learner, Facilitator, Assessor, Moderator, Super User; every screen adapts to the role
  3. Audit logging — every significant action (submissions, messages, deletions, sign-offs) is recorded with actor and timestamp
  4. Account lifecycle — email sign-up, password recovery, admin promotion/demotion, remove-profile-from-device

Platform capabilities

  1. Runs everywhere — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop; installable as an app (PWA) with offline support
  2. Real-time cloud sync — work saved on one device appears on every other; local cache keeps the app usable with no signal
  3. Zero-install updates — new features deploy centrally; every user gets them on next refresh
  4. Dark & light themes, print-optimised styling, and full keyboard/browser accessibility patterns

Capabilities in learners’ hands

  1. Learn, practise, get marked and submit evidence from one app — on their phone
  2. Instant marks with reasons, plus AI that understands answers in their own words
  3. Progress that updates itself the moment work is done
  4. Private chat to the facilitator; class Q&A; motivating XP & leaderboard
  5. Digital signing of the attendance register in seconds
  6. Forms completed once online — never re-written on paper

Capabilities in admins’ hands

  1. See every learner’s real answers, activity and risk status live
  2. Automatic marking and progress capture — zero admin typing
  3. Audit-ready evidence: registers, portfolios, logbooks and declarations printable on demand
  4. One-click CSV / PDF / ZIP exports for SETA & QCTO reporting
  5. Broadcast to the cohort; moderate any conversation read-only
  6. Role-controlled access with a full audit trail behind every action
ITSS Learn can run the entire learnership digitally: it teaches, marks, tracks, communicates, reports and audits on its own.
