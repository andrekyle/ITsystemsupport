Conclusion

The July 2026 System Support training programme progressed satisfactorily, with Module 5 completed as planned and only one module remaining. Learners continued to build relevant knowledge and practical skills in server systems and peripherals, while most attended regularly and participated positively in the training sessions.

Teamwork and assessment

The group generally demonstrated good teamwork, respect, and commitment to learning. All 14 learners submitted work, which is currently undergoing assessment. Attendance and punctuality concerns were limited to a small number of learners.

Attendance follow-up

In some cases, absences were linked to illness and were communicated to the facilitator, while other absences and late arrivals took place without timely notice. These cases should continue to be monitored.

Outstanding submissions and assessment follow-ups should remain a priority, while learners are encouraged to maintain regular attendance and communicate any challenges as early as possible.
