System Support · September 2026

Summary

The System Support programme continued to demonstrate positive progress as learners advanced through the final stages of Module 6. Learners strengthened their knowledge and practical skills in server administration, computer peripherals, and Database Access. The majority of learners remained actively engaged and showed commitment to completing programme requirements. A significant achievement is that learner portfolios and assessments have been submitted and are progressing through the assessment process.

Resilience and collaboration

Several learners displayed resilience by balancing training commitments with personal, health, family, and employment-related responsibilities. Strong teamwork, peer support, respectful conduct, and participation contributed to a productive and collaborative learning environment. Learners who maintained excellent attendance consistently demonstrated dedication and a positive attitude towards learning.

Growth and attendance

The group has shown measurable growth in both technical competence and professional development throughout the programme. Attendance and punctuality remained areas requiring closer attention, as some learners experienced challenges in maintaining consistent participation. In certain instances, missed sessions and limited communication affected continuity of learning and progress tracking.

Despite these challenges, learners continued to demonstrate determination and a willingness to overcome obstacles. Continued focus on outstanding activities, logbooks, and assessment requirements will ensure readiness for completion. The cohort can be proud of its growth, knowledge, and experience, despite one learner being dismissed.

Overall progress reflects commitment to skills supporting future employment opportunities. With October graduation approaching, there is a strong sense of excitement, accomplishment, and anticipation as learners prepare to celebrate qualification completion and new opportunities.
