This quotation sets out the pricing, scope and terms for the development, hosting/maintenance retainer, optional developer services, and contract terms for the ITSS Learn Learning Management System ("the App") supplied to Eruditio Skills Development Consultants (PTY) LTD ("the Client").

1. Once-off Development — Administrative Build

A once-off development fee to build the LMS application to the Client's administrative requirements (learner management, course administration, reporting and related back-office functionality).

The build includes role-based access for staff. A staff member can be a Facilitator, Assessor and/or Moderator in any combination — one person may hold all three roles, while others may be only a Facilitator, only an Assessor, or only a Moderator. Each user's access to lesson plans, assessment marking and moderation functions follows the role(s) assigned to them.

  1. The monthly application fee of R 10 000.00 (R 120 000.00 per annum) is payable annually in advance, before any new Client development request is performed.
  2. The monthly fee covers the ongoing operation, maintenance and support of the App only.
  3. The monthly fee EXCLUDES: domain registration/renewal fees, database hosting fees, and AI model/API usage fees. These are billed separately at cost, or may be paid for directly by the Client.
  4. New development requests raised by the Client outside of the agreed scope are quoted and invoiced separately, and are only carried out once the current annual application fee has been paid in full.

3. Delivery, Onboarding & Timeline

  1. Delivery and onboarding of the App is scheduled for 25 September 2026.
  2. Recommended timeline: 4 months from onboarding to complete all training materials and administrative documentation, depending on the final number of courses the Client wishes to upload. This estimate should be re-confirmed once the course list is finalised.

5. Contract Term & Renewal

  1. This quotation, once accepted, forms the basis of a 3 (three) year service contract between the Vendor and the Client.
  2. The contract is renewed every 12 (twelve) months, subject to both parties confirming continuation in writing prior to each renewal date.
  3. Pricing may be reviewed and adjusted at each annual renewal, with any changes communicated to the Client in writing at least 30 days before the renewal date.
