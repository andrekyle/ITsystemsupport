## 📚 Content Structure

### **Lesson 1: Introduction to Technical Practitioners Meetings**
- **Duration:** 90 minutes (Self & Group Activity)
- **Specific Outcome:** Demonstrate knowledge of different types of technical practitioners meetings
- **Key Topics:**
  - Importance of meetings for collective decision-making
  - Types of technical meetings (contract, technical review, project review)
  - Leadership styles (democratic, autocratic, facilitative)
  - Decision-making processes (voting, consensus, criteria-based rating, ranking, paired comparisons)
- **Quiz Questions:** 5
- **Assessment Criteria Addressed:** AC 1-5 of SO 1

### **Lesson 2: Different Types of Technical Practitioners Meetings**
- **Topics:**
  - Contract Meetings
  - Technical Review Meetings
  - Project Review Meetings
- **Quiz Questions:** 3

### **Lesson 3: Leadership Styles in Meetings**
- **Topics:**
  - Democratic Meetings (delegate-based, participatory)
  - Autocratic Meetings (chair-controlled, directive)
  - Facilitative Meetings (group effort, participatory leadership)
- **Quiz Questions:** 3

### **Lesson 4: Decision-Making Processes in Meetings**
- **Topics:**
  - Voting (majority rule, supermajority)
  - Consensus (full agreement or acceptance)
  - Criteria-Based Rating (scored evaluation)
  - Ranking (preference ordering)
  - Paired Comparisons (pairwise preference)
- **Quiz Questions:** 4

### **Lesson 5: Meeting Conventions and Procedures**
- **Topics:**
  - Moving (formal proposal)
  - Seconding (supporting motions)
  - Amending (modification of motions)
  - Voting Procedures (counting and recording votes)
  - Note Taker Technical Background Knowledge
- **Quiz Questions:** 4

### **Lesson 6: Preparing for a Technical Practitioners Meeting**
- **Duration:** 90 minutes (Self & Group Activity)
- **Specific Outcome:** Prepare for a technical practitioners meeting
- **Key Topics:**
  - Venue Selection (size, accessibility, location, facilities)
  - Facilities (chairs, tables, flipcharts, AV equipment)
  - Technology Requirements (internet, video conferencing, software)
  - Supporting Information (guides, references, background materials)
  - Meeting Outcomes (clarity, documentation, communication)
  - Participant Invitations (timeous notification)
  - Agenda Distribution (advance preparation)
- **Quiz Questions:** 4
- **Assessment Criteria Addressed:** AC 1-4 of SO 2

### **Lesson 7: Chairing a Technical Practitioners Meeting**
- **Duration:** 90 minutes (Self & Group Activity)
- **Specific Outcome:** Chair a technical practitioners meeting
- **Key Topics:**
  - Role of the Chairperson (pace-setting, decision-making, fairness)
  - Establishing Rules and Guidelines
  - Applying Meeting Conventions
  - Following the Published Agenda
  - Managing Active Participation and Conflict
  - Handling Difficult Behaviors:
    - The Heckler (aggressive, argumentative)
    - The Overly Talkative Person (controlling discussion)
    - The Cynic (complainers, pessimists)
    - The Silent Member (shy, uncertain, disengaged)
    - Clashing Egos (personality conflicts)
    - Side Conversations (off-topic discussion)
    - Factually Wrong Statements (misinformation)
  - Prioritizing Topics and Allocating Time
  - Ensuring Clear Decisions with Timeframes and Accountability
- **Quiz Questions:** 4
- **Assessment Criteria Addressed:** AC 1-6 of SO 3

### **Lesson 8: Conducting Post-Meeting Follow-Up for a Technical Meeting**
- **Duration:** 90 minutes (Self & Group Activity)
- **Specific Outcome:** Conduct post-meeting follow-up for a technical meeting
- **Key Topics:**
  - Importance of Post-Meeting Support (implementation, decision quality)
  - Decision Implementation Planning
  - Follow-Up of Implementation Activities
  - Support for Interaction Between Decision Makers and Implementers
  - Awareness Support to External Members
  - Accurate Minute-Taking (documentation standards)
  - Communication of Decisions (format, timeliness, distribution)
  - Summary of Discussions and Actions (clarity, responsibility, timeframes)
- **Quiz Questions:** 4
- **Assessment Criteria Addressed:** AC 1-3 of SO 4
