5. Platform Features by Role

The App provides the following capabilities, organised by the role each serves. Access is governed by the role(s) assigned to each user (Facilitator, Assessor and/or Moderator, in any combination).

Administrator

  1. User & access management — create accounts and assign the Facilitator / Assessor / Moderator roles (one person may hold all three, or only one).
  2. Live cohort dashboard — completion, quiz & exercise averages, attendance rate, last-seen and XP for every learner in one sortable table.
  3. Early-warning engine — automatically flags at-risk learners with the reasons (low completion, poor attendance, inactivity, low scores).
  4. Answer transparency — open any learner and see every option picked and every sentence typed.
  5. Audit-ready exports — one-click CSV / PDF / ZIP of registers, portfolios and the analytics table for SETA & QCTO reporting.
  6. Audit trail — every significant action (logins, submissions, marking decisions, messages, edits, deletions and sign-offs) is recorded with the actor, timestamp and affected record, retained for the contract period and available for review or export to support SETA / QCTO audits and POPIA accountability.
  7. Conversation moderation — open any conversation read-only, enforced by the database.

Student / Learner

  1. Interactive lessons — slide-based lessons with images, pop-up glossary definitions, worked examples and collapsible sections.
  2. Auto-marked assessments — multiple choice, select-all, drag-to-order and matching, with instant scoring and best-score tracking.
  3. AI marking of written answers — understands answers written in the learner's own words and gives credit for meaning, with explanatory feedback.
  4. Evidence upload — submit photos, PDFs and documents from any device into private cloud storage.
  5. Automatic progress — completion updates instantly as work is done, with XP, levels and a class leaderboard.
  6. Digital attendance & forms — sign the register and complete official forms online; never re-written on paper.
  7. Private chat & Q&A — message the facilitator and join class questions, from phone, tablet or computer.

Facilitator

  1. Classroom presenter mode — full-screen slides with speaker notes to teach the material live.
  2. Content management — upload slide decks, images and lesson figures that sync to every device.
  3. Knowledge gating — lock the next slide until learners answer checkpoint questions correctly.
  4. Marking oversight — review auto-marked and AI-marked answers, and unlock self-assessments for learners.
  5. Messaging & broadcast — private 1-to-1 chat with each learner plus one-to-many announcements to the whole class.
  6. Attendance & registers — capture and print attendance registers and paper-form replicas that auto-fill from stored data.
