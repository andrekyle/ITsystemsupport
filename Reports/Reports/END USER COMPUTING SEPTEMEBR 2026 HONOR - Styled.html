End User Computing · September 2026

Summary

During September 2026, training for the NQF Level 3 End User Computing programme focused on Microsoft Excel and Outlook. Of the 16 learners enrolled, two were dismissed following continued concerns regarding poor attendance and academic submissions. This was an unfortunate and difficult outcome, as every learner began the programme with the opportunity to grow and succeed. However, the decision was necessary to uphold the programme’s standards and to protect a focused, committed learning environment.

Resilience and progress

The departure of two members of the group did not diminish the morale or engagement of the remaining learners, who continued to show resilience, determination and mutual support. Fourteen learners have submitted work, which is currently being assessed.

Graduation outlook

The programme has provided learners with a valuable platform to strengthen their academic, digital and workplace skills, while also developing greater confidence in their abilities. As graduation approaches, there is a genuine sense of excitement and pride among the learners.

The occasion will recognise not only the achievements of those who successfully complete the programme, but also the commitment, perseverance and personal growth demonstrated throughout their learning journey. Eruditio is proud to have partnered with Honor in delivering this meaningful initiative and looks forward to sharing a memorable graduation celebration with the learners and stakeholders, honouring the progress made and the opportunities that lie ahead.

Conclusion

The End User Computing programme showed encouraging and measurable progress during September 2026. Four structured training sessions were successfully delivered, supporting continuity in learning and allowing learners to build further on their practical skills. A strong level of participation and commitment was evident across the group.

Skills and assessment

During the month, learners continued developing their practical knowledge of Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Outlook. These skills remain important for both workplace readiness and successful programme completion. Learner work was also submitted for assessment, marking an important step towards completion.

Support and resilience

Although two learners were dismissed during the reporting period, the remaining group continued to show resilience, focus, and a positive attitude towards their studies. Class morale remained strong, with growing excitement around the upcoming graduation ceremony.

Continued attendance monitoring, regular communication, and targeted facilitator support should remain a priority. Graduation will provide an opportunity to recognise the learners’ commitment, growth, and achievements throughout the programme.
